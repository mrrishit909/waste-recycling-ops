Waste Recycling Ops

An invented district of 500 smart containers on a Saturday with a festival on. Overflow risk from each container's own fill rates, dynamic routes against the fixed schedule, a toy conveyor classifier that says it is one, and material sent where it is worth most within what each plant can take. …
The district, its containers, readings, trucks, facilities, prices and conveyor frames are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real city, sensor or image. The classifier is a toy on schematic frames. No outside model or service is called.
